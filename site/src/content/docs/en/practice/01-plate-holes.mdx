## Setup

1. Launch FreeCAD 1.1.3. Check the Report view for `[CAD X-ray] 서버 시작 http://127.0.0.1:9877 (툴 36개)`.
2. File → New. Keep exactly one document open.
3. In a terminal, go to your working folder and open your AI tool (`claude`, `codex`, `gemini`).

## Steps

### 1. Check the connection

```text title="Say this"
Check whether you can reach FreeCAD and tell me the name of the open document.
```

**What the AI does** — reads the FreeCAD version and the open document.

:::note[What the AI says back]
Connected to FreeCAD 1.1.3. Open document: Unnamed (0 objects).
:::

### 2. Make the plate

```text title="Say this"
Draw a 100×60 plate 5 thick on the XY plane and pad it upward. The origin is the bottom-left corner of the plate. Put the dimensions in a spreadsheet called Params as width, height and thickness. Show me an iso screenshot when you're done.
```

**What the AI does** — creates a Body, draws a rectangle sketch on XY with every constraint applied (DoF 0), and pads it 5 mm. The three dimensions go into the `Params` spreadsheet as aliases, and the sketch and the Pad reference those cells. It recomputes, confirms there are no errors, and shows you a screenshot.

**How to check** — the tree gains `Body › Params, Sketch, Pad`. Double-click the sketch and the status bar at the bottom says "fully constrained". Close to leave.

:::tip[If no screenshot appears]
Codex and Gemini may summarise images as text. Look at the FreeCAD window directly with View → Standard views → Axonometric (key `0`).
:::

### 3. Drill the holes

```text title="Say this"
Drill four Ø6.6 through holes, 10 in from each corner. Use Params.hole_d for the diameter and Params.margin for the corner distance.
```

**What the AI does** — creates a sketch with four circles at the top face height (centres at (10,10), (90,10), (10,50), (90,50)), sets the diameter constraint to the expression `Params.hole_d`, and cuts them through with a Pocket. It recomputes and confirms again.

**How to check** — the tree gains `Sketch001, Pocket`. Double-click `Params` and you see five rows (width 100, height 60, thickness 5, hole_d 6.6, margin 10).

### 4. Confirm with numbers

```text title="Say this"
Tell me the volume and the sketch degrees of freedom. And how many holes there are and whether they go through.
```

:::note[What the AI says back]
Volume 29,316 mm³ (plate 30,000 − 684 for four holes). Both sketches at DoF 0. Four Ø6.6 holes, all through, rectangular pattern 80×40.
:::

Subtract the hole volume (π × 3.3² × 5 × 4 ≈ 684) from 30,000 and you get the right number.

### 5. Change a dimension — what parametric means

Do this one yourself, without the AI. Double-click `Params` in the tree, change `thickness` from 5 to 8, and recompute (F5, or Edit → Refresh). The plate gets thicker and the holes still go through. Put it back to 5.

You can have the AI do it too.

```text title="Say this"
Change margin to 12 in Params, recompute, and check nothing newly broke.
```

### 6. Save

```text title="Say this"
Save it to D:\cad-work\plate.FCStd.
```

**How to check** — the window title changes to `plate` and the file appears at that path. The AI does not save before you say this.

## When it doesn't work

- **"Cannot connect"** — FreeCAD is closed or the server did not start. See [Troubleshooting](/cadxray/en/setup/03-troubleshoot/).
- **The AI asks which document** — close the others or give it the name.
- **The holes ended up outside the plate** — you did not state the origin, so the plate got centred on the origin. Add "the bottom-left corner of the plate is the origin" and ask again.
- **The sketch is not "fully constrained"** — say "the Sketch still has degrees of freedom. Find what's missing and show me candidates." Same flow as [Exercise 6](/cadxray/en/practice/06-fix-sketch/).
- **The plate went downward** — say "reverse the Pad direction".

## Going further

1. Add R5 fillets on the four vertical edges and C0.5 chamfers at the hole mouths.
2. Instead of Ø6.6, just say "clearance holes for M6 bolts" and see what diameter the AI picks.
3. Take this plate through [Exercise 7](/cadxray/en/practice/07-drawing-pdf/) and produce a three-view drawing.
