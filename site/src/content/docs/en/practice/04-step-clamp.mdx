A vendor STEP is a lump with no features, so you cannot change its dimensions. This exercise drives the whole cycle — **read, measure, restack as features, compare against the original** — by talking. In the original session the volume difference came out at +0.035 mm³ (0.0004 %), a verdict of identical.

## Setup

1. Launch FreeCAD 1.1.3. You want `[CAD X-ray] 서버 시작` in the Report view.
2. Put the STEP at `C:\cad\04-tt_fractal_vise_assembly.step`.
3. In PowerShell, move to `C:\cad` and open `claude`.
4. Do not open a document first. The AI creates one when it opens the STEP.

## Steps

### 1. Open the STEP and get the analysis table first

```text title="Say this"
Open C:\cad\04-tt_fractal_vise_assembly.step and analyse the part labelled 2B2: structure and validity, holes (diameter, depth, through, counterbores, chamfers, pitch), face classification and whether it can be rebuilt, band boundary z values, and a table of datum points (measured from vertices) versus design values.
```

**What the AI does** — four things in turn. ① Opens the STEP into a new document and reads the part hierarchy. It is an assembly, so 80 parts come in; the internal name of an object imported from STEP is `Part__Feature…` and the original name is in the Label. ② Groups concave cylindrical faces by axis and centre to count holes, attaches counterbores and chamfers on the same axis to their hole, and matches diameters against the ISO metric thread table. ③ Judges the identity of each face; BSpline faces get sampled and fitted against cylinders and cones, so "fake free-form" faces get filtered out. ④ Reads the band, groove and lip z boundaries from a histogram of vertex coordinates, keeps datum points (X0=20.343193, Y0=45.052724, Z0=53.679659) as measured, and tidies design values such as thickness 12 and height 16 to integers.

:::note[What the AI says back]
80 parts of 20 types. 2B2 is Part__Feature004, 1 solid, 44 faces (8 BSpline), valid.
Holes: one Ø3.4 through (counterbore Ø5.6 depth 4.0, 0.5 chamfer at the mouth, estimated M3 clearance), two Ø2.2 pin holes 5.0 deep (pitch 28.5). Through/blind is a heuristic.
Faces: verdict prismatic, main axis Z. 11 planes · 22 cylinders · 11 cones (the 8 BSplines resolved as cones), 0 free-form. Radii 35.0 (barrel) / 33.620391 (groove floor) / 17.75 (seat) / 16.12127 (lip).
Band boundary z: 53.68 (bottom) / 57.39 / 57.97 / 65.39 / 65.97 / 69.68 (top).
Datum (measured): back face x 20.343193, centre hole y 45.052724, bottom z 53.679659. Design values: thickness 12, height 16, hole height bottom+8.
:::

:::caution[Radii and datum points are never rounded]
Write 33.620391 as 33.6204 and the mating faces are off by 9e-6, and the fuse loses 0.6 mm³. The summary table (`rebuild.radii`) is rounded to three decimals, so have the AI put the values from the face details into the parameters at full precision.
:::

**How to check** — 80 parts in the tree and no errors in the Report view. Proceed if the verdict is `prismatic` or `mixed` (with `free_form`, go the BaseFeature route: lay the original down and stack features on top). Three holes (one Ø3.4 plus two Ø2.2), and a table that clearly separates measured values (six decimals) from design values (integers). From here on, put "2B2" in every sentence so the target is unambiguous.

### 2. Have it redraw the part

```text title="Say this"
Redraw 2B2 as a Body in a new document jaw_2B2. Every dimension in Params, radii and datum points exactly as measured. Verify after each feature and stop and tell me if one fails.
```

**What the AI does** — builds the 12 features as a single JSON list and stacks them in order. The three bands are traced by cutting the original section directly into a sketch (lower → middle → upper, overlapping), so the coordinates never travel through the conversation. The barrel dovetail groove is a revolved cut, and the ear the groove removed is re-padded from what lies outside the groove radius. Two revolved cuts make the seat-lip transition wedges, then the front ear is restored. Holes use the step 1 values as Pockets (Ø3.4 through, two Ø2.2 5 deep, counterbore Ø5.6 depth 4) with a 0.5 chamfer at the mouth. It recomputes after each feature and stops at the one that fails.

:::note[What the AI says back]
PadBand1 → PadMid → PadBand2 → GrooveBarrel → PadEars → GrooveLip1 → GrooveLip2 → PadSeatEars → PocketHole → PocketPins → PocketCbore → ChamferHoles. All 12 Valid, all 12 sketches at DoF 0, volume 9,200.33 mm³ (original 9,200.30).
:::

**How to check** — all 12 features under the Body should be in black text, and the `Params` sheet should hold x_back, thickness, barrel_r, groove_r, hole_d, cbore_d and the rest. If it stops partway, the AI gives you the name and status string of the feature it stopped at, so you only fix that one.

### 3. Compare against the original and save

```text title="Say this"
Compare it against the original 2B2: volume difference and any missing or extra fragments. If identical, show me an iso view and save to C:\cad\jaw_2B2.FCStd.
```

**What the AI does** — takes a fuzzy boolean difference between the two shapes and gives you the volume difference and the bboxes of the missing and extra fragments. The verdict is identical (under 0.001 %), match (under 0.1 %) or different.

:::note[What the AI says back]
Volume difference +0.035 mm³ (0.0004 %), verdict identical. No missing fragments; four extra fragments of 0.01 mm³ each, tolerance-level slivers where the lip transition wedges sit. Saved.
:::

**How to check** — `identical` or `match` is a success. With `different`, the fragment bboxes are exactly where you went wrong; ask it to fix only the feature at that location.

| Criterion | Value |

| Features | 12 (5 Pads, 3 Grooves, 3 Pockets, 1 Chamfer), all Valid |
| Sketch DoF | all 12 at 0 |
| Volume difference | +0.035 mm³ (0.0004 %) |
| Comparison verdict | identical |
| Time (rebuild tool calls) | about 2.0 s (including 5 section traces), 0.78 s to compare |

## When it doesn't work

- **The verdict is different and the fragments are near the barrel groove** — you rounded the groove radius. Say "put groove_r back to the measured 33.620391 and redo it".
- **A Pad fails saying it is separated and cannot go in one Body** — the band Pads do not overlap. Ask for each band's height to be extended into its neighbour by the transition height.
- **It says the free-form area is large** — go hybrid: lay the original down as a BaseFeature and stack only the holes and grooves as features. Say "keep the original as a BaseFeature and make only the holes parametric".
- **The comparison reports boolean_failed** — overlapping faces defeated the boolean. Judge by volume and area difference alone in that case.
- **You received an STL or OBJ** — with no faces and no solid, this exercise's hole and comparison methods do not work. Go to Exercise 5 (redrawing an STL).

## Going further

1. Change `Params.hole_d` to 4.5 (M4 clearance) and recompute, and see whether the counterbore and chamfer follow.
2. Run an interference check over the whole assembly: "check whether any pair of parts overlaps". In the original session, 3,160 pairs across 80 parts were checked in 6.5 seconds — zero interference, nine touching pairs.
3. Ask for the mass: "how much does it weigh in aluminium (2.7 g/cm³)?"
4. Redraw the other 19 types the same way, then rebuild the whole assembly with one Body per type and 80 Link instances placed from it (3.5 minutes in the original session; of the 20 types, 15 identical and 4 match). The order is `examples/rebuild_*_with_tools.py` → `assemble_from_bodies.py`.
