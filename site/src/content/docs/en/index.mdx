## What this site is

Attach **cadxray** to FreeCAD 1.1.3 and AI coding tools — Claude Code, Codex CLI, Gemini CLI — can read, build and fix the FreeCAD document you have open. This site is not documentation for that connector. It teaches you **how to build 3D models by talking to it**. Every page has «Say this» boxes: copy the sentence, paste it into your AI tool.

## The order we suggest

1. Finish **Install** and **Connect an AI tool**. Thirty minutes.
2. Do **Exercise 1** — four holes in a plate. Once a model comes out, you are ready.
3. Pick the exercise closest to your own work. Have a drawing? 2, 3 and 10. Vendor parts? 4 and 5. An existing model that keeps turning red? 6.
4. When you get stuck, read **Writing prompts**, rewrite your sentence, and ask again. Questions go to [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/WaveSimm/cadxray/issues).

## Things to know up front

- All traffic stays inside your PC (127.0.0.1). Your model files never leave the machine. Your AI tool, however, does send the conversation to its vendor's servers.
- The AI **does not save files until you tell it to.** When you like what you see, say "save it".
- Fix candidates always come back as a numbered list, and only the ones you pick get applied. Nothing is fixed automatically.
- Everything here was verified on FreeCAD 1.1.3 · Windows. On macOS and Linux only the install paths differ; the steps are the same.
