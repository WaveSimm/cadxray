This is the review you do before the slicer: is the model printable? The AI checks whether walls are thinner than the nozzle, whether it can be printed without supports, and whether it fits the bed — and fixes what can be fixed using PartDesign features (to undo, delete the feature). It does not slice.

## Things you can ask for

| Say this | What you get |
|---|---|
| "Check it for PETG with a 0.6 nozzle, no supports" | A score and a list of problems (thin walls, overhangs, bridges, small holes, horizontal holes, bed fit, first-layer adhesion), each with severity, location and how to fix it |
| "Which way should I orient it?" | Six directions plus the current one, ranked by support area, bed contact and height. Pick one and the rotation is applied |
| "Apply the elephant foot and hole compensation fixes" | A chamfer on the bottom outer edges, and a Pocket enlarging vertical holes by the compensation value |
| "Teardrop the horizontal holes" | A 45° teardrop above each horizontal hole |
| "Thicken the thin walls" | Pads thin planar walls outward to the minimum thickness (this changes the outside dimensions) |
| "Add wedges under the overhangs" | Fills below planar overhangs attached to material, at the limit angle |
| "It's bigger than the bed, cut it up" | Splits into pieces along an axis and hides the original (joining pins are manual) |
| "How many grams, how many hours, how much?" | Material volume, weight, filament length, time and cost |
| "Paint the problem faces" | Thin walls red, supports orange, bridges yellow, bed contact green |

## A representative prompt

```text title="Say this"
Review the Body 'SpoolGuide' for PLA, 0.4 nozzle, 0.2 layer, bed 220×220×250.
Show me the problems as a numbered list, and for anything fixable just show me the candidates. Don't fix anything automatically.
```

:::note[What the AI says back]
Profile: PLA · nozzle 0.4 · layer 0.2 · bed 220×220×250 (minimum wall 0.8, overhang limit 45°). Print direction +Z, floor at z=0.

Score 62/100. Fits the bed. First-layer contact area 3,120 mm².

1. Thin walls — four ribs at 0.6 mm (< 0.8), at (35, 0, 12) and three others · severe · fix: thicken
2. Overhang — 10 mm cantilever, support area 240 mm² · moderate · fix: overhang_chamfer or reorient
3. Small holes — Ø0.6 × 2 (< 0.8) · moderate · redesign
4. Horizontal hole — Ø6 × 1, will sag at the top · minor · fix: teardrop

Fix candidates: thicken (1), overhang_chamfer (2), teardrop (4), elephant_foot (bottom 0.3), hole_comp (vertical holes +0.2). Pick the numbers you want. Reorienting could eliminate 2 — shall I show you the ranking as well?
:::

From there it goes "show me the orientation ranking" → a candidate table → "use the second one" → the rotation is applied → "check it again".

## The profile

You state the material (PLA, PETG, ABS, ASA, TPU, Nylon), nozzle, layer height and bed size. Leave them out and it uses PLA · 0.4 · 0.2 · 220×220×250 and says so. Minimum wall thickness, overhang limit angle, maximum bridge and hole compensation come from a table of empirical values (roughly slicer defaults and manufacturer guidance), not precise figures. If you know your own printer's numbers, override them: "minimum wall 1.0, overhang 50°".

## Things to know

- **Automatic fixes change the geometry.** `thicken` grows walls outward, so outside dimensions change; wedges add material, so the weight changes. Re-check and confirm your dimensions after applying.
- **Curved thin walls and curved overhangs are manual.** The automatic fixes only handle planar geometry.
- **Split pieces are created outside the Body.** Joining pins and dowels you add yourself.
- **Time and material estimates are around ±30 %.** Coarser than a slicer. Give it a price per kg and it works out the cost.
- **FDM prints are weak along the layer direction.** If you are going as far as structural analysis, read the limits in [Structural analysis](/cadxray/en/features/08-fem-assembly/) alongside this.
- Non-Body objects (a STEP lump) can be checked but not auto-fixed. Move them into a Body first, or use [Redrawing](/cadxray/en/features/03-rebuild/).

## What runs behind the scenes

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `check_printability` | Checks thin walls, overhangs, bridges, holes, bed fit and adhesion against the profile; `issues`, `fixes`, `paint` |
| `suggest_orientation` | Scores six directions plus the current one; `apply` rotates |
| `apply_print_fixes` | elephant_foot · hole_comp · teardrop · thicken · overhang_chamfer · split |
| `estimate_print` | Volume, weight, filament, time, cost |
