Leave FreeCAD open, tell the AI the dimensions, and it draws the sketch and stacks Pads, Pockets and fillets in order to build a Body. It replaces picking a sketch plane with the mouse and adding constraints one at a time. The result is an ordinary FreeCAD PartDesign model — turn the AI off and you can still open the features in the tree and edit them by hand.

## Things you can ask for

| Say this | What you get |
|---|---|
| "Make a 100×60×10 plate" | A Body with an XY-plane rectangle sketch + a 10 mm Pad |
| "Ø6.6 through holes 10 mm in from each corner" | A four-circle sketch + a through Pocket. The diameter becomes the parameter `hole_d` |
| "A Ø40 cylinder 30 mm tall with a Ø20 boss 5 mm on top" | Two Pads stacked, overlapping, in one Body |
| "Cut a groove by revolving around the centre axis" | A revolved cut (Groove) — the AI picks the section polygon and the axis |
| "Revolve this section 360°" | A Revolution |
| "R3 fillet on the outer edges, 0.5 chamfer at the hole mouths" | Fillet and Chamfer, with edges selected by radius and position |
| "Ø44.5 male thread, pitch 2, length 8" | A helical cut — one pitch of the root section swept as a helix |
| "Pull the thickness out as a parameter" | An alias appears in the `Params` spreadsheet and the feature references that expression |

## A representative prompt

```text title="Say this"
Make me a bracket in a new document.
- Base plate 120×80, 8 thick
- Four Ø6.6 through holes, 12 mm in from each corner of the base plate
- A vertical plate on the rear long edge, 60 tall and 8 thick (an L shape)
- A Ø20 through hole in the middle of the vertical plate
- R5 fillets on the outer edges
Pull every dimension out as a parameter, and show me an isometric screenshot when you're done.
```

:::note[What the AI says back]
Confirmed the FreeCAD 1.1.3 connection. Created document `Bracket` and stacked five features in the Body.

1. PadBase — 120×80 rectangle, 8 mm (sketch DoF 0)
2. PocketHoles — Ø6.6 × 4, through (`Params.hole_d`)
3. PadWall — 60 mm vertical plate on the rear edge
4. PocketCenter — Ø20 through
5. FilletOuter — R5, 6 edges

Volume 79,432 mm³, no Invalid objects. Parameters are in the `Params` spreadsheet as `width`, `depth`, `thickness`, `wall_h`, `hole_d`, `center_d`, `fillet_r`. Isometric screenshot attached. I have not saved.
:::

Check the screenshot and the tree to confirm you got what you asked for. Dimensions you dislike can be changed as numbers in the `Params` spreadsheet, or you can just tell the AI "make wall_h 80".

## What parametric means here

Every sketch the AI builds has zero remaining degrees of freedom, and the dimensions reference spreadsheet aliases. Which means:

- Double-click `PadBase` in the tree and change the length by hand, and the features below follow.
- Change `hole_d` from 6.6 to 9 in the `Params` sheet and all four holes grow at once.
- The sketches the AI creates are hidden in the tree (`Visibility=False`) but are otherwise ordinary sketches. You can open them and edit the constraints.

:::tip[Stack them overlapping]
Pads inside one Body have to touch or overlap. FreeCAD refuses two separated lumps in a single Body. For "separate parts", ask for the Body to be split in two.
:::

## Things to know

- **You have to ask it to save.** The AI does not write the file until you say "save it". Give it the name and the path.
- **Give it the datum plane and direction and it won't ask back.** Something like "base on XY, up is +Z". If you leave it out, the AI decides and tells you what it decided.
- **Break long jobs up.** Put dozens of features in one sentence and, when one of them fails midway, it is hard to find where it stopped. Saying it in three passes — mass, then holes, then finishing (fillets and chamfers) — is safer.
- **A failed feature stays in the document.** When the AI says "stopped at PocketCenter", tell it the cause (the sketch is outside the shape, say) and have it delete and rebuild.
- **The AI reports whether volume went up or down** for each feature. If you cut a hole and the volume did not drop, the Pocket never met any material.

## What runs behind the scenes

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `build_features` | Stacks a list of sketches + Pad/Pocket/Groove/Revolution/Fillet/Chamfer/helix into a Body in order, verifying each feature; `params` go into a Spreadsheet |
| `tracked_recompute` · `get_screenshot` | Confirms the recompute result; PNGs from 12 directions including iso and front |
| `save_document` | Saves only when asked |
